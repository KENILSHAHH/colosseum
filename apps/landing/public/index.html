Trust & security

Autonomy for the agent. Control for you.

Real funds on mainnet. These are the rules the agent works inside.

Your keys stay split

Your wallet is a Privy wallet. Its key is kept in parts and is never whole on our servers.

Revoke at any time

The agent signs through a signer you added. Remove it and the agent can no longer act.

You choose the mode

Confirm every action on a card, or let the agent act on its own within a limit you set.

Simulated first

Each transaction runs on live chain state before it is sent. A failing plan never reaches the chain.

Scoped permissions

For your own wallet, a permission names the contracts, the functions and an end date. Nothing else is allowed.

Verified contracts

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is PayI?

A chat app with one AI agent for DeFi. You say what you want, for example "open a 3x ETH long with a stop" or "buy 10 USD of ETH every day", and the agent plans it, tests it on live chain data and executes it from your wallet.

Which chains and apps does it support?

Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Polygon, Robinhood Chain, HyperEVM, Hyperliquid, Solana and Tempo. Venues include Hyperliquid, Aave v3 and v4, Morpho, Kamino, Jupiter, Polymarket, Relay and Jumper. For anything without a shortcut, the agent builds the transaction itself.

What does it cost?

0.01 USD per prompt, paid in OUSD on Tempo. Gas on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Polygon and Robinhood Chain is paid by our sponsor. Venue and bridge fees apply as usual.

Who holds my keys?

Your wallet is a Privy wallet: its key is kept in parts and never whole on our servers. The agent signs through a signer you added, and you can remove it at any time.

Can the agent act without asking me?

Only if you turn on "act without asking". Otherwise each action waits on a card for your confirmation. Scheduled jobs run on their own once you confirm them.

What are the risks?

This runs on mainnet with real funds. Leverage can be liquidated, a fast market can pass a stop, and new contracts carry risk. Nothing here is financial advice. Start with a small amount.
